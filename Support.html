Frequently Asked Questions

When will Signstimator be available?

We’re currently building the iOS and Android versions. Join the notification list on the home page and we’ll email you as soon as the first public release is ready.

What types of signs will the estimator cover?

Channel letters, vinyl graphics, vehicle wraps, banners, dimensional letters, and more. The goal is a practical tool built specifically for sign shops and fabricators.

Will it work offline?

Yes — estimating calculations are designed to work on-device so you can price jobs even without a strong internet connection.

Can I customize pricing and markup?

Absolutely. You’ll be able to set your own material costs, labor rates, markups, and company branding (including logos) so the estimates match how your shop actually works.
