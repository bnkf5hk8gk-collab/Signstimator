Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Signstimator (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Signstimator mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use the App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Signstimator is designed to perform calculations primarily on your device. We collect only the minimum information necessary.

Information You Provide

  • Contact information (such as name and email address) if you submit a form on our website or contact us directly.
  • Any feedback or support requests you send us.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (for crash reporting and analytics, if enabled).
  • Usage data: how you interact with the App (screens viewed, features used) — collected only if you consent to analytics.
  • Crash and performance data: to help us improve stability.

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or any personal content you enter into the calculators unless you explicitly share it with us for support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App
  • To improve features, performance, and user experience
  • To respond to support requests and feedback
  • To send important service-related notices (e.g., major updates)
  • To detect and prevent technical issues or abuse

3. Third-Party Services

We may use limited third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, Apple App Store analytics or similar privacy-focused tools). These services process data only as necessary to provide their functionality and are bound by their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising networks or track you across other apps or websites for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Calculator inputs and results are stored locally on your device by default. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information we process. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact and support information only as long as necessary to respond to you or as required by law. Analytics and crash data are retained for a limited period solely for product improvement.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), the European Economic Area (GDPR), and other jurisdictions have additional rights that we will honor.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place where required.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
