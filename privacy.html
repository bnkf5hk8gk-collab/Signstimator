Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Signstimator (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Signstimator mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Core Principle: Your Data Stays on Your Device

Signstimator is designed as an on-device estimating tool. All calculations, estimates, pricing information, customer details, logos, notes, and any other data you enter or create in the App are stored only on your device.

We do not collect, receive, transmit, store, or have access to any of this data on our servers or systems. We never harvest your estimating data, pricing details, customer lists, logos, or any content you create inside the App.

2. Information We Collect

Data that stays only on your device

  • All estimator inputs and results (channel letters, vinyl, wraps, banners, dimensional signs, etc.)
  • Pricing information and calculation details
  • Saved estimates, jobs, and project history
  • Customer information you enter
  • Company or customer logos you upload
  • Any notes or settings you create

This information never leaves your device unless you personally choose to share it (for example, by emailing an estimate, exporting a file, or otherwise sending it to someone).

Information you may choose to send us

  • Contact information (name and email) if you email us or submit a form on our website
  • Any feedback, support requests, or messages you send us

We only receive this information if you actively send it to us.

Limited technical information (if enabled)

  • Basic device information (model, operating system version) and crash reports — only if you consent to analytics or crash reporting
  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed or features used) — only if you opt in

These are optional and used solely to improve app stability and performance. They do not include your estimates, pricing data, customer information, or any identifying content from the App.

3. Your Responsibility for Data Stored and Shared

Because all estimating data, pricing information, customer details, and related content remain solely on your device, you are fully responsible for that data.

  • Any information saved or stored within the App is under your control.
  • If you export, email, share, or otherwise transfer any data (including pricing details, estimates, or customer information) to another person or entity, that information is then in the hands of the recipient and outside our control.
  • We are not responsible for any data stored or saved within the App, nor for what happens to information after it has been exported or shared by you.

Please take appropriate care when sharing sensitive business or pricing information.

4. How We Use Information

  • To respond to support requests and feedback you send us
  • To improve the App’s stability and performance (only with optional technical data)
  • To send important service-related notices if needed

We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, or selling.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use limited privacy-focused tools for crash reporting or basic analytics (for example, Apple’s own App Store analytics). These services only process technical data needed for their function and are bound by their own privacy policies.

We do not use advertising networks. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Storage and Security

All estimator data, pricing information, customer details, logos, and related content remain on your device under your control. We implement reasonable measures to protect any limited information you choose to send us (such as support emails).

7. Data Retention

Because we do not receive your App data, we have nothing to retain. Any contact or support information you send us is kept only as long as needed to respond to you or as required by law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you (for example, support emails). To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. Residents of California, the European Economic Area, and other jurisdictions have additional rights that we will honor.

For data stored only on your device, you control it completely — you can delete it by removing the data inside the App or uninstalling the App.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have received such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@signstimator.com
Website: https://signstimator.com

This Privacy Policy is intended to meet the requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. It accurately reflects that Signstimator is an on-device application, that we do not receive or store your estimating or pricing data, and that you remain responsible for any data you store in or export from the App.
